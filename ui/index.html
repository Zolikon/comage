Keyboard shortcuts

j / kNext / previous comment
a / AApprove / approve with comment
dDecline
iIgnore for now (stays open, comes back next round)
uUndo decision
rResolve / reopen
yCopy suggestion
cComment on selected text
EscClose popover / form

Every action autosaves. A round ends in one of three ways:
Submit round hands your comments and decisions to Claude to fix.
AI review has the reviewers you pick read the document; your pending items wait.
Approve makes this version final and closes the review, and this tab with it; whatever is still open is left as it is.
